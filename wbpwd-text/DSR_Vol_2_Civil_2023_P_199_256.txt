@249 FOREWORD
@54 Delhi Schedule of Rates published by Central Public Works Department is a comprehensive
@54 and useful technical document of various items used for execution of civil works. In addition
@54 to CPWD, the Schedule of Rates are used by many Central and State Government
@54 Departments, Institutions, Public Sector Undertakings, Architects and Contractors.
@54 Delhi Schedule of Rates was first published in the year 1931, followed by numerous
@54 revisions/publications from time to time in order to bring it in line with the prevailing new
@54 building materials, current technologies, market rates and for implementation of revised
@54 tax regimes. It was revised in 1977, 1981, 1985, 1989, 1993, 1997, 2002, 2007, 2010
@54 (Reprint), 2012, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2019 and 2021.
@54 The last edition of Delhi Schedule of Rates was published in the year 2021 and its revision
@54 had become necessary owing to:
@73 a) @93 Provision contained in CPWD Manual to revise it after every two years.
@73 b) @93 Incorporating Corrections in items which issued through correction slip No.1 to 21.
@73 c) @93 Revision of GST rates on works from 12% to 18% w.e.f 18.07.2022.
